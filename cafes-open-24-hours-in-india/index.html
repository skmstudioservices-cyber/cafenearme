Are there 24-hour cafes in India?

Quick answer: A few, mostly in the big metros and mostly near transport hubs, hospitals and IT parks. Most cafes close by late evening, so if it is late, filter the map rather than guessing.

More questions

Which Indian cities have 24-hour cafes?

Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune have the most late-night options. Smaller cities usually have none outside transport hubs.

Where can I sit late at night in India?

24-hour cafes, hotel coffee shops, and food courts at large petrol pumps and airports are the dependable options. Hotel coffee shops are open around the clock in most metros.

Are 24-hour cafes safe at night in India?

Choose lit, staffed places with other customers — hotel coffee shops and mall food courts are the safest defaults. Avoid isolated roadside places after midnight.
