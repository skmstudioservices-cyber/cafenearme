What to check before you go

Open the map, tap the pin and read what is recorded for that cafe — the fee, the seating, the opening hours where tagged. Where a detail is missing the page says so instead of guessing, which is more useful than a confident wrong answer.

Use the filter for places open now, or check the opening hours on the listing before you walk. Small cafes often close mid-afternoon.
