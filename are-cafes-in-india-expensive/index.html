Are cafes in India expensive?

Quick answer: It depends on the cafe, not the city. A neighbourhood cafe serving filter coffee or chai usually costs a fraction of a chain outlet in a mall, and both are on the same map.

More questions

What is the cheapest kind of cafe in India?

Small neighbourhood and roadside cafes, and the tea stalls that also serve snacks, are almost always the cheapest. Mall and chain outlets cost more for the same drink.

Do cafes in India charge extra for service?

Most cafes do not add a service charge. Some add GST, and some add a service charge that you can ask to have removed.

Is a cafe cheaper than a restaurant in India?

Usually yes for a drink and a snack. A cafe bill is typically much smaller than a full restaurant meal for the same number of people.
