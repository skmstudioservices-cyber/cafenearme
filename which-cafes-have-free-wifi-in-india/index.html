Which cafes in India have free wifi?

Quick answer: Most chain cafes and most mall-based cafes offer free wifi to customers. Small standalone cafes often do not. The reliable way to check is to look at the listing before you go.

More questions

Do I need to buy something to use cafe wifi?

At almost every cafe, yes — the wifi is for paying customers. Ordering one drink is the normal courtesy.

Is cafe wifi in India safe?

Treat public cafe wifi as public. Avoid banking and anything with a password you would not shout across the room; mobile data is safer for that.

Do cafes in India have charging points?

Most chain and mall cafes do, usually along the walls or under the tables. Small cafes often have one or two sockets, and it is fine to ask.
