Where cafes cluster in Chennai

Most cafes in Chennai sit in a handful of areas: T. Nagar, Anna Nagar, Adyar, Velachery and Nungambakkam. High streets have the density, malls have the predictable chains, and business districts have the ones that stay open longest. If you are new to the city, start with the high street and walk — you will pass several.
