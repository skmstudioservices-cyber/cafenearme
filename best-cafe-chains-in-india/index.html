Which cafe chains are in India?

Quick answer: India has a deep set of national and regional cafe chains, from coffee-first brands to bakery-cafes and tea chains. Which one is nearest matters far more than which one is best.

Which coffee chain has the most outlets in India?

The large national coffee chains have the widest spread across metros and tier-2 cities. Use the map to see which one is actually near you rather than assuming.

Are local cafes better than chains in India?

Often for food and price; chains are more predictable for wifi, seating and card payment. Most people end up using both.

Do cafe chains in India have vegetarian food?

Yes — vegetarian and egg-less options are standard at Indian cafe chains, and most mark them clearly on the menu.
