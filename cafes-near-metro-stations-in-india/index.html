Are there cafes near metro stations in India?

Quick answer: Yes, and they are the easiest cafes to reach without a car. Metro stations in the big cities almost always have cafes inside the complex or within a short walk of an exit.

More questions

Do metro stations in India have cafes inside?

Many do, especially the newer and larger interchange stations. Airport and railway metro stations are the most reliable.

Which metro city has the most cafes?

Delhi and Bengaluru have the widest cafe spread around their metro networks. Mumbai's are concentrated near the business districts.

Are cafes near metro stations open early?

The ones inside stations usually follow the first train. Standalone cafes near stations typically open mid-morning.
